## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please submit your vulnerabilities using [Malwarebytes' bug bounty program](https://malwarebytes.com/secure).

You will be kept in the loop, and publically credited for discovering the issue (you may choose to stay anonymous).

## Third party code

If you discover a vulnerability in third party code, please report it to their respective maintainers.
